Consumer Protection Notice for DeKalb Seniors & Veterans: Do NOT pay any private company or contractor upfront for a "BrighterHome qualification inspection." Under Decide DeKalb and DeKalb County program rules, initial intake qualification is 100% paperwork-based. Once your deed, residency, tax, and income documents are approved, Decide DeKalb assigns a pre-qualified program inspector/contractor to evaluate your home and write the official scope of work at $0 cost to you.

1. DeKalb BrighterHome Rehab Program Up to $30,000 Forgivable

Administered by Decide DeKalb Development Authority (decidedekalb.com). Forgivable loan (100% forgiven after 10 years of continued primary occupancy) for critical roof, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, and accessibility repairs.

Paperwork to Gather Before the Portal Opens:
  • 10+ Years Primary Residency & Clear Title: Recorded warranty deed in your name (minimum 10 consecutive years owner-occupied; priority for 15+ years). Resolve any deceased spouse or heir-property probate clouds now.
  • Household Income ≤ 80% AMI: Tax returns, W-2s, Social Security, or pension award letters showing combined household income at or below 80% Area Median Income (priority for <60% AMI, seniors 62+, veterans, and disabled residents).
  • Current DeKalb Property Taxes & Mortgage: Verification from the DeKalb Tax Commissioner that property taxes and any mortgage are paid current.
  • Active Homeowner's Insurance Declaration Page: Programs require active hazard insurance coverage in force on the property.
  • No Prior SPHRP Grant in Past 10 Years: Confirm you have not received DeKalb Special Purpose Home Repair ($14K CDBG) funds within the last 10 years.

2. DeKalb HomeStart & Retail Inspections Up to $20,000 Buyer DPA

Also funded by the $155M Housing Bond, DeKalb HomeStart provides up to $20,000 (with enhanced tiers for DeKalb public servants, teachers, and first responders) in 0%-interest, 10-year forgivable down payment and closing cost assistance.

When You SHOULD Book Foresight ($345+):

  • Legacy Owners Deciding to Sell Instead ($345+): Over the 80% AMI income cap, or do not want a 10-year residency lien on your equity? Book a Pre-Listing Seller Inspection with regional contractor cost data before listing.
