🔍 Top 7 Concealed Defects Foresight Catches on New Construction

These defects occur routinely across major national builders and custom builds throughout Metro Atlanta:

🪓 1. Severed Structural I-Joists

Plumbers and HVAC crews frequently notch, drill, or cut through engineered floor joists and load-bearing studs to route lines, compromising floor stiffness and structural load paths.

🌪️ 2. Missing Hurricane Ties & Clips

Unfastened or improperly nailed hurricane clips connecting roof trusses to top wall plates, leaving the roof vulnerable to uplift during high-wind storms.

💨 3. Disconnected Attic HVAC Ducts

Unstrapped flexible ductwork pinched over rafters or detached at plenums, blowing air conditioning directly into attics while pulling fiberglass dust into living areas.

🚿 4. Unflashed Shower Pan Membranes

Improperly sloped pan liners and unsealed corners beneath tile, causing subfloor decay, mold growth, and lower ceiling leaks within the first 12-24 months.

⚡ 5. Missing Nail Protection Plates

Subcontractors skipping steel nail plates where electrical wiring or PEX plumbing passes through studs, risking puncture when drywall screws are driven.

❄️ 6. Missing Insulation Behind Tubs

Cavities behind bathtubs, stairwells, and fireplace chases left completely uninsulated before fixtures are set, creating permanent thermal voids and freezing pipes.

🧱 7. Reverse Grading & Clogged Weeps

Exterior brick veneer weep screeds covered by sod or concrete, and soil sloped toward foundation footers, trapping Georgia red clay moisture against the slab.
