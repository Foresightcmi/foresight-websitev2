1. Legal Requirement & Closing Mandate

Under DeKalb County Code of Ordinances Section 25-41 et seq., any residential real estate transfer in unincorporated DeKalb County or participating municipalities requires an executed Certificate of Compliance with Water Conservation Standards prior to closing.

The closing attorney and title underwriter must receive a signed, stamped compliance certificate certifying that all toilets, showerheads, and sink faucets meet county flow rate limits. Transfer of title without this certificate can result in closing escrow holdbacks ($500 to $1,500), water billing transfer rejection, or county compliance notices.

3. Statutory Flow Rate Benchmarks

To qualify for the official DeKalb County Certificate of Compliance, all installed plumbing fixtures must meet or exceed the following flow thresholds:

Plumbing Fixture Type Maximum Permitted Flow Rate Testing Standard Status
Water Closets (Toilets) 1.60 GPF (Gallons Per Flush) Tank stamp or volumetric calibration test Mandatory
Showerheads 2.50 GPM (Gallons Per Minute) Calibrated flow meter test @ 80 PSI Mandatory
Lavatory Faucets (Bathroom) 2.00 GPM (Gallons Per Minute) Calibrated flow gauge sweep Mandatory
Kitchen Sink Faucets 2.20 GPM (Gallons Per Minute) Calibrated flow rate sweep Mandatory

*Note: Ultra-high efficiency fixtures (1.28 GPF or WaterSense® certified 1.5 GPM showerheads) exceed statutory minimums and fully satisfy compliance requirements.
